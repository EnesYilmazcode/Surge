Everything happens on one 3D board

Each part is placed into real sockets, so you can see exactly what is connected to what.

  • Snap

    Each part drops onto the sockets under it and clicks home.

  • Wire

    Click two sockets or a battery terminal. The jumper arcs over the board.

  • Run

    The solver works out the voltages and currents, then lights the LEDs.

  • Keep

    Circuits autosave in your browser. Download a .surge file to share one.
